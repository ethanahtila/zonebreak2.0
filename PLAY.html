ESC Match menu

You have three ammo cells. They reload automatically. Stop attacking and avoid damage for four seconds to regenerate health. Supers charge through damage and healing. Tanks also charge from enemy damage taken. Only Rook, Bulwark, Wisp and Nyx charge slowly over time. After purchasing hypercharge at level 20 for 2,500 coins, it needs three super charges worth of combat energy; press F when ready for eight seconds of boosted speed, reload, basic damage and an upgraded super. Gadgets activate once per cooldown after unlocking. No gadgets or passives are active at level 1. Gadgets unlock at levels 5, 10, 15 and 18; passives at levels 8, 12, 16 and 20. Reaching an unlock level does not give you the ability: purchase it with coins, then equip one gadget and one passive. Levels also cost coins; there is no XP. Fighters start at 65% of their maximum health, damage and healing. These stats increase every level and reach full strength at level 20.

Choose a class to see its fighters. Rarity sets credit cost and mastery complexity; it is not a blanket stat bonus. Each has different stats, attack patterns and a unique super. Rook has almost no attack range. Jolt has mid-range burst damage. Sprig’s shots heal wounded teammates or hurt enemies. Fuse lobs over walls. Wisp is fast and fragile. Marksmen have medium health and very long-range shots, but cannot shoot through walls. Bushes conceal enemies unless they are close or reveal themselves by attacking or taking damage.

Hot Zone is 3 vs 3 with respawns; a contested zone gives no progress. Duel and Knockout have no respawns during rounds. The first team to two round wins wins. A closing danger ring prevents endless hiding; a round timeout compares remaining health.

Start with Jolt; collect credits from the trophy road and lootboxes. Recruitment rolls a rarity and up to three unowned candidates from that rarity. Choose a target, then future credits fill its requirement automatically. Legendary lootboxes can still unlock a fighter directly. Controllers hold areas with fields, returning shots and destroyable turrets. Some tanks, damage dealers and artillery can break walls. Marksmen and assassins cannot; Controllers can only do it with a marked gadget, never a super. Wins give 8 fighter trophies, draws 4 and losses 2. The first five wins each day earn a six-click lootbox. Daily win lootboxes reset at midnight in Cedarville, Ohio. Progress is stored on this browser, not a server account. Online queues require 2 or 6 real players with exactly the same selected-fighter trophy count, regardless of level. Friends rooms can start with fewer humans and add bots. Disconnecting players are replaced by bots so the match continues.

LOOTBOX ODDS & REWARDS

Six clicks total. Each click attempts one upgrade: Common → Rare 45%, Rare → Epic 25%, Epic → Mythic 12%, Mythic → Legendary 5%. Failed attempts keep the current rarity.

Coins: Common 60–100; Rare 120–180; Epic 240–360; Mythic 500–750; Legendary 1,000–1,500. Only Legendary has a 25% chance to award a locked fighter directly. Other payouts have a 25% chance to be credits instead of coins: 30 / 70 / 150 / 300 / 500 by lootbox rarity. Credits automatically advance your selected recruit. Earned, unopened lootboxes remain available after daily reset.

FIGHTER RECRUITMENT

Choose your next fighter.

New credits automatically go to this target. Surplus credits carry over when the fighter unlocks. Finish the target before choosing another.

Common: 100 credits · Rare: 250 · Epic: 500 · Mythic: 900 · Legendary: 1,500. Higher rarities demand more technical play, not automatically stronger stats. If fewer than three fighters remain in a rarity, all remaining choices are shown.
